- Preserve staking policy, wallet switches and the wallet/chain lock order.

- Preserve callback order and manager/worker lifetimes.
- Preserve EvoDB transaction, rollback and disk-commit boundaries, together with coin-view consistency.
- Preserve Qt balance fields, nonblocking refresh, selections and user actions.
